The Horologist Profile

Master of Horology since 2014

The Soul Behind Time Point

My journey into watch curation began in 2014. What started as a small personal passion for fine timepieces soon became a lifelong commitment to quality and authenticity in the heart of Multan.

Click to read our full story & experience ↓

For more than a decade, I have focused on sourcing and servicing authentic watches—a discipline that demands patience and an eye for detail no machine can replicate. Every timepiece at Joni Baba Store is hand-selected and verified to ensure it carries the spirit of true craftsmanship.

Today, we stand as a symbol of heritage in Pakistan. Our goal is to provide watches that don't just keep time, but develop a beautiful story with every year you wear them.

Expertise: Watch Authentication, Movement Servicing, Bespoke Sourcing.
